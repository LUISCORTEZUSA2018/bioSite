Her Story

Kristen values genuineness, simplicity, and attention to detail. Her roots are in Louisiana, and she also spent meaningful time in Colorado, Costa Rica, and the Dominican Republic.

Her childhood included time with family in the country, fishing, playing outside, and singing with her sisters. Her parents taught her the importance of hard work, respect, and honesty.

Faith Image

Faith & Growth

Kristen considers her Christian faith the foundation of her life. It has sustained her through difficult seasons and guides her relationships, decisions, growth, and future goals.

She hopes to continue deepening her faith and understanding of the Holy Spirit while investing in and encouraging the people around her.

Education Image

Education & Accomplishments

Kristen completed a one-year theological Training Program at her church in Dallas, studying for two semesters alongside a cohort of women who challenged and strengthened her faith.

She speaks Spanish and taught high school Spanish for 15 years. She is also working to expand her knowledge of Special Education processes and hopes to advocate for parents of Special Education and Section 504 students.

Looking Forward

Kristen hopes to continue growing in faith and character. In the future, she hopes to be married with children and to become more loving, faithful, kind, and patient.

“I would want them to remember that while I’m not perfect, it was the grace of God that sustained me in life’s hardest seasons.”
