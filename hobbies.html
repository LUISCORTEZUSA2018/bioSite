Music & Worship

Music has been part of Kristen's life since childhood. She grew up surrounded by hymns and music and received lessons in piano, guitar, and violin.

In her small country church, she learned to sing harmony by listening to the congregation sing in four-part harmony. Music and worship remain closely connected to her faith.

Reading Image

Reading & Learning

Kristen enjoys reading many different genres. As a child, she especially enjoyed fiction and mystery books and the feeling of becoming part of a story.

As an adult, she enjoys theology, Christian living, writings from early church leaders, memoirs, and books that challenge her thinking and deepen her understanding.

Travel & Life Experiences

Traveling is another interest that has been part of Kristen's life. Her experiences in places including Louisiana, Colorado, Costa Rica, and the Dominican Republic have contributed to her perspective and personal story.
